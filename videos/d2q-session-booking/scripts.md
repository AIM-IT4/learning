# Desk2Quant session-booking reels: scripts

Format for all three: faceless motion graphics (Ripping-type), 45–60 s, Charon narrator, 2–5-word kinetic captions in
the top third with the key word in teal, a new picture every 1–2 s, light paper and dark navy scenes alternating,
Desk2Quant logo pill at the top, comment-keyword CTA at the end.

Rules kept: no prices, discounts, codes or "free"; no bank or employer names; numbers only from the site's own
public stats (50 mentees, 5.0 from 25 reviews); simulated screens are labelled.

Each comment keyword needs an auto-DM rule (ManyChat or the Instagram API) that replies with the booking link:
https://desk2quant.com (sessions section).

## 1. "The follow-up" — Risk Quant Mock Interview (pilot, produced)

Hook built on the account's best performer ("You can write Black–Scholes from memory. Then the interviewer asks…",
69K views on 20 Oct).

| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| Hook | You can write Black–Scholes from memory. Then the interviewer asks: what happens if vol doubles? And your mind goes blank. |
| Call-out | If you've got quant interviews coming up, this one's for you. |
| Reframe | Reading more notes won't fix that moment. Interviews don't test what you know. They test the follow-up: the second question, asked under pressure. |
| Reveal | So practise the follow-up. Book a one-on-one mock interview on Desk2Quant. |
| How it works | Forty-five minutes, live, with a practising quant. It runs like the real thing: probability, stats, stochastic calculus, risk models and Python, with the follow-ups real interviewers ask. You're scored on clarity, correctness, speed and depth. Then a debrief: your fixes, in priority order, and a study plan for the next two to three weeks. |
| Objection | Not ready for a full mock? Start with a fifteen-minute call on your doubts, or a thirty-minute deep dive on your resume. |
| Guest mentors (v3) | Want a specialist's view? Book a guest mentor: one-on-one sessions on credit risk, Basel and IFRS 9. |
| Proof | Fifty mentees so far. Rated five out of five. |
| Tip | Quick tip: answer out loud, then answer again in half the time. If you can't, you don't own it yet. |
| Line to remember | Notes teach you the answer. A mock teaches you to say it. |
| CTA | Comment MOCK and we'll DM you the booking link. |

Caption: You can derive it. Can you defend it? 🎯 45-min 1:1 mock interview with a practising quant: probability,

## 3. "The crossover" — Resume deep dive

| Beat | Line |
|---|---|
| Hook | Your quant resume says "backtested a moving-average crossover." So does everyone else's. |
| Reframe | Interviewers don't read your project. They read what it says about the desk you'd join: the risk you measured, the model you challenged, the number you'd defend. |
| Reveal | In thirty minutes, one-on-one, we rewrite it to sound like the desk. |
| How it works | We pick the two projects worth keeping, turn each bullet into a result with a number, and script the first two minutes of "walk me through your resume." You leave with an action plan and the resources to finish it. |
| Proof | Fifty mentees so far. Rated five out of five. |
| Tip | Quick tip: every bullet should answer "so what?" in the same line. |
| Line to remember | Your resume gets you the call. Your story gets you the offer. |
| CTA | Comment RESUME and we'll DM you the booking link. |
