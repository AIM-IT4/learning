# Desk2Quant reel templates

The three approved Instagram reel styles (1080 × 1920, HyperFrames 0.9.39). The rules for each style are in
`AIM-IT4/desk2quant`, `.claude/CLAUDE.md`, under the "Reels" sections. Read them before starting a reel.

| Template | Style | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `d2q-day11-rip` | Ripping-type motion graphics | Daily concept reels (Day N/30): one big hero per beat, the caption is the voice |
| `d2q-mr-promo` | Chapter promo | Product promos and any reel that walks through a list, in 6–8 chapters |
| `d2q-day12-cva` | Code-along lesson | "Build it in Python" lessons in pricing, risk and XVA that fit in about 16 lines |

Start a new reel by copying the template's folder, then follow its `README.md` and `BRIEF.md`. Each `BRIEF.md` is the
production brief of the first reel made with that template, so the paths in it (such as `../d2q-day11-hud` or
`/home/user/Films/...`) point to that reel's original working folders: reuse the method, not the paths.

Voice takes, music renders and finished videos are not kept in this repository.
