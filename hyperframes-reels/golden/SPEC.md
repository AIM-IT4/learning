1080×1920, 30 fps, 26.9 s. Voice: Manish (saved, consented voice; Audio8). House palette: paper, ink, teal, yellow, coral.

Script: एक, एक, दो, तीन, पाँच, आठ, तेरह… हर संख्या, पहले की दो संख्याओं का जोड़। इन्हें वर्गों में बनाइए, और जोड़ते जाइए… एक सर्पिल
बनता है। अब हर बीज को, पहले वाले से, एक सौ सैंतीस दशमलव पाँच डिग्री घुमाकर रखिए… और देखिए, सूरजमुखी बन गया!
प्रकृति भी, गणित से चलती है।

Beats: the numbers 1 1 2 3 5 8 13 pop in as spoken (Poppins 112 px, 128 px apart) → a coral bracket slides along the row with the
sums 1 + 1 = 2, 2 + 3 = 5, 5 + 8 = 13 (three, each held over a second) → "वर्गों": squares 1 … 34, each added on the next side in turn
(right, down, left, up), numbered, while the camera eases out to keep the whole rectangle in frame (34 × 55 at 16 px a unit) → the golden
spiral, one quarter arc per square, drawn in yellow over ink → the squares fade → seeds: seed n sits at angle n × 137.508° and distance

zoom keeps the head 300 px across → "सूरजमुखी": a dark head, seeds striped by n mod 34 (so the spirals show), 2 × 34 petals bloom →
"φ = 1.618…" replaces the title, with 360° ÷ φ² = 137.5° beside it. One canvas, redrawn from timeline time.

sparkle for the spiral, clicks for the first seeds, chime for the bloom.
