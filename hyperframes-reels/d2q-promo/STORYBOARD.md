## Logline
A quant aspirant can derive Black–Scholes but can't hedge with it. One notebook shows the difference: the hedging
error falls from 2.32 to 0.07. Desk2Quant is introduced as the place that trains the desk side. It ends on all 10
free resources by DM.

## What we borrow (technique only, nothing copied)
- One sentence, one scene: a word-by-word caption on top and one glossy object that acts the sentence out.
- Scene backgrounds alternate on every cut, joined by pixel-staircase wipes. A drifting-squares motif runs throughout.
- Hero-word slam: the line dims while one word lands huge, then shrinks back into its place.
- A rolling counter as the proof. A coin flip on the mirrored tagline.
- The persuasion arc: hook challenge → who it's for → proof number → reframe → reveal → First / Then / Then →
  objection → mirrored tagline → "comment and we'll DM you".
