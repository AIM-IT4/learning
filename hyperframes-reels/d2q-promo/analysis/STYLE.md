## In one line
A fast first-person voiceover sells one product in 19 shots. Every sentence gets its own scene: a word-by-word caption at
the top, and under it one glossy object that acts the sentence out as a visual metaphor. Scenes alternate light and dark
every cut, joined by pixel-dissolve wipes, and it ends on a "Comment X and I'll DM you" card.

## Editing
- 19 shots; mean 3.58 s (7.7 beats at 129 BPM); shortest 1.77 s, longest 6.47 s.
- Cuts land on sentence ends, not on beats. The voice drives the edit.
- **Transitions:** at every cut (17 detected as flash frames), a pixel-dissolve wipe. Either a jagged staircase edge
  of square pixels sweeps across, or the old scene breaks into floating squares and the new one assembles from them.
  The same pixel squares float in the background of every scene as a motif.
- **Camera:** moving in 63% of shots, always slow. Push-ins dominate, with a few pull-outs and one orbit. Nothing is
  fast except the counters and the hero-word slams.

## Colour and look
- **Light scenes:** grey-white (#ECECEC) with white glossy cards, black text and a green accent.
- **Dark scenes:** charcoal (#2A2A2A) with a soft neon-green radial glow behind the subject, white text and a green
  accent.
- One brand colour only: a neon green (~#22C55E / #1ED760). Red appears once (a drop marker); orange appears in a
  🔥 emoji and on the launch page.
- **Objects:** glossy, rounded, product-render props on a pedestal or floor shadow (silver coin, padlock, anchor, claw
  machine, hourglass, ring light). Each one is a literal metaphor for the sentence.
- The brand logo (icon and wordmark) sits small at the top centre of every frame.

## Text (the lead character)
- **Caption** in the top 15%, 1–3 centred lines, in a heavy geometric sans with tight tracking. Words appear one at a
  time on the voice (fade and rise with a slight blur). One or two words per sentence turn green.
- **Hero-word slam:** for the key word, the sentence dims to 25% grey. The word ("3.3", "million", "homework",
  "buyers", "product.", "audience.", "free.") slams in at about 3x size in green over it, holds about 0.4 s, then
  shrinks back into its place in the line.
- **Numbers:** rolling counters with scrambling digits (3,300,000; 100,000), plus big standalone numbers ("10",
  "100", "10x").
- Emoji inline as colour accents (🚀 🔥 ⚡ 📷).

## Story structure (how it persuades)
hook claim → audience call-out with a promise → personal proof number → "and that isn't even the craziest part" →
reframe the real problem → product reveal → how it works (First… Then…) → "nothing copied" → choice (100 styles) →
objection: camera → meta proof (this video was made with it) → mechanism (shown only to non-followers, 10x sooner) →
learning loop → cost objection → bonus tip → a two-line tagline in mirrored form → "just launched, test it free" →
comment-for-DM CTA. Motif: the tool's icon recurs on every prop. Bookend: it opens on what the tool does and closes
on the tool itself.
