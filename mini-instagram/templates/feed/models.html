3 · Suggested for you a recommender: friends of friends

If several people you follow all follow someone, you probably know them. No training: just counting over the follow graph. Brand-new users get the most-followed accounts instead.

4 · Explore a recommender: item-item collaborative filtering

Two posts are "similar" when the same people liked them. Explore shows posts from accounts you don't follow that are most similar to what you already liked.
